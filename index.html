Public Key Auth

RSA, ECDSA, Ed25519 — generate, import, or paste. Your private keys never leave the device.

Terminal Colors

Full ANSI 256-color support. Your tmux, htop, and vim look exactly right.

Encrypted Storage

Credentials stored in Android EncryptedSharedPreferences. Hardware-backed when available.

Save connections with labels, ports, usernames, and key bindings. One tap to connect.
