Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

LeuwiPanjang ("we", "our", or "the app") is an open-source, lightweight SSH client and terminal emulator for Android developed by Rolly Maulana Awangga. We are deeply committed to user privacy, data security, and transparency.

1. Zero Data Collection

LeuwiPanjang does not collect, harvest, transmit, or store any personal information, telemetry, usage statistics, or analytics. We do not track your activity, your device identifiers, or your network traffic.

2. SSH Credentials and Key Storage

All SSH connection details (including hostnames, IP addresses, port numbers, usernames, passwords, and private SSH keys) are stored strictly locally on your Android device within the app's sandboxed private storage.

  • No credentials or private keys are ever uploaded to any third-party cloud servers, relays, or external proxies.
  • Connections are established directly via SSH protocols (Port 22 or custom port) from your device to your destination server.

3. Permissions Required

LeuwiPanjang requires only the minimal permissions necessary to operate as an SSH terminal:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required exclusively to open outbound TCP sockets to your designated SSH servers.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely for optional tactile feedback upon key presses.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Used optionally to prevent the screen from sleeping while an active SSH session is running.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

LeuwiPanjang does not incorporate any third-party advertising frameworks, tracking SDKs, commercial analytics libraries, or third-party marketing services.

5. Open Source Code Transparency

The complete source code of LeuwiPanjang is open and publicly available on GitHub at https://github.com/leuwipanjang/leuwipanjang. You are free to audit and inspect the code at any time.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository or at awangga@gmail.com.
